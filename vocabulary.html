Practical Vocabulary

The words you'll actually reach for first — greetings, numbers, family, and everyday phrases — plus the one number rule that catches every learner off guard.

The rule nobody expects: gender polarity
Numbers 3–10 reverse gender agreement: a feminine noun takes the masculine-looking form of the number, and a masculine noun takes the feminine-looking form (the one ending in ة). It looks backwards compared to every other agreement rule in the language, precisely because it is — Arabic calls this deliberately-opposite behavior "polarity", and it only applies to this one number range.
